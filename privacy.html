Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

IS COMPANY (LED Mirror & Construction Services, Lahore, Pakistan) operates this website and app to show wholesale mirror rates and receive customer orders.

Information we collect

When you place an order, we collect the information you type in: your name or shop name, your city or delivery address, and the products and quantities you choose. We do not ask for passwords, payment card details or any other sensitive information from customers.

How we use it

We use this information only to receive, confirm and deliver your order, and to contact you about it. We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Orders are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Access to orders is restricted to the owner of IS COMPANY. When you tap the WhatsApp button, your order message is sent through WhatsApp, which has its own privacy policy.

Accounts

Customers do not need an account. Sign-in is used only by the business owner to manage products and prices.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your order information at any time by contacting us on WhatsApp.
